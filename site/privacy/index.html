Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

LumaDocs is a personal document vault for Android and iOS. It lets you store, organize and find your own documents — identity papers, travel documents, medical records and similar — using your own Google Drive account as storage.

The short version. LumaDocs has no servers of its own. Your documents are stored on your device and in your Google Drive account. The developer cannot see, read, collect or retrieve your documents, and no document content is ever transmitted to the developer or to any third party.

1. Who is responsible

This app is published by Husanboy Roziqov ("we", "the developer"), an independent developer. For any question about this policy or your data, contact husanboyroziqov7@gmail.com.

3. Google Drive access

LumaDocs requests exactly one Google Drive permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file.

This is Google's narrowest Drive scope. It grants access only to the files LumaDocs itself creates, and to files you explicitly open with LumaDocs. The app cannot see, list or touch any other file in your Google Drive. We deliberately do not request the broader drive or drive.readonly permissions.

Limited Use disclosure

LumaDocs' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide and improve features visible to you in the app; it is not transferred to others except as required to operate those features or as required by law; it is not used for advertising; and no humans read it, except with your explicit permission, for security purposes, or to comply with applicable law.

4. Where your data is stored

There is no LumaDocs server, backend or database. Your data lives in exactly two places:

  • On your device — a local cache of your documents and previews, your settings, and your app lock PIN (stored in encrypted form).
  • In your own Google Drive — under your Google account, governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

The app communicates only with Google's own endpoints (googleapis.com and drive.google.com). It sends data to no other host.

5. Analytics

The Android version includes Google Firebase Analytics, which automatically collects basic, non-identifying usage information: app opens, screens viewed, session length, device model, operating system version, general language/region, and a randomly generated app instance identifier. This is used only to understand crashes and general usage. It never includes your documents, their names, or their contents. See Firebase Privacy and Security.

6. Device permissions

  • Camera — only to photograph a document when you tap the scan button. There is no background camera use.
  • Photos / media — only to let you pick an image to add to your vault.
  • Notifications — only for local reminders that a document you saved is about to expire. These are generated on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

Every one of these is optional. Declining a permission disables only the feature that needs it.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your documents or personal information with third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or build advertising profiles.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

8. Your control over your data

  • Delete individual documents — from within the app, or from your Google Drive.
  • Clear local data — uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your device settings. This removes the local cache, settings and PIN.
  • Revoke Drive and account access — at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access does not delete files already in your Drive; you control those directly in Drive.
  • Request help — write to the contact address above.

Because the developer never holds a copy of your data, deletion is entirely in your hands: removing files from your Google Drive and uninstalling the app removes everything.

9. Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS. Documents you mark as encrypted are encrypted on your device before being uploaded. The optional app lock PIN is stored in encrypted form and never transmitted. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we do not operate a server that could be breached to expose your documents.

10. Children

LumaDocs is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
