Android & iOS

Your documents,
kept safe.

LumaDocs is a private vault for the papers that matter — passports, tickets, medical records. Stored in your own Google Drive. Never on our servers, because we don't have any.

Your Drive, your files

Documents live in your own Google Drive account. We use Google's narrowest permission, so the app only ever touches files you add through it.

No servers, no copies

There is no LumaDocs backend. Nothing you save is transmitted to the developer or to any third party.

Locked down

Optional PIN lock, and on-device encryption for documents you mark as sensitive before they ever leave your phone.

Never miss an expiry

Set an expiry date and get a local reminder before your passport or visa runs out. Reminders are generated on your device.

Scan and go

Photograph a document straight into the vault, sorted automatically into Identity, Travel or Medical.

Find it fast

Search across names and descriptions, filter by category, and browse full-quality previews without leaving the app.
